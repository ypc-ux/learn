## Why These Emails Work (A16Z Rules Applied)

| Rule | How |
|---|---|
| Subject: curiosity, under 6 words | "saw your reviews, built you something" / "your competitor has a better website" |
| Line 1 = context lean-in | Matt's: references the delay (personal, human). Dustin's: who I am + proof |
| No corporate speak | "I know I said yesterday" not "I apologize for the delay in responding" |
| Contractions everywhere | "I'd" "That's" "don't" |
| Under 90 words | Matt: ~65 words. Dustin: ~55 words |
| One ask | "What day works this week?" |
| Proof link in email | Link to the live site (visual proof > words) |
| Sounds like a person texted it | Short sentences. No periods stacking. No "I am writing to" |
| Confidence, not apologetic | "I wanted it to be perfect" (states the reason, doesn't grovel) |
