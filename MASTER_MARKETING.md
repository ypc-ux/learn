## 5. THE TROJAN HORSE PLAYBOOK (condensed)

1. Extract a slice of the real paid service (valuable alone, doable in <1 week, needed by all ICPs).
2. Pitch only the gift. No payload in first touch.
3. Overdeliver like it's the $10K project.
4. Let them ask "what else can you help with?" — that's the close.
5. The gift must be real. Fake gifts burn the market forever.

Full version: `WEB_DESIGN_BIBLE.md` → Trojan Horse Marketing. Sources: Bustamante ($10K first client via this method), Farnam Street mental model, Berger's Contagious (stories as vessels).

**Our pitch template (proven structure):**

> Subject: Your competitor booked the lead you missed
>
> Hey [Name] — quick one about [Business].
>
> Most contractors call leads back in 42 minutes. The average homeowner has hired someone else by minute 10.
>
> But here's the thing: we built a system that calls your leads in 5 minutes, pre-qualifies them, and books them on your calendar.
>
> One roofing company in Conyers got 20 booked appointments in 30 days. Paid $200 per appointment that showed up.
>
> Worth 15 minutes to see if it works for you?
>
> — Julius

Rules: subject under 6 words · under 90 words total · one ask · no links in email 1 · Day 3 follow-up = one new fact (never "bumping this") · Day 7 = case study · stop at 3 touches.

## 6. COPY RULES (the short list)

- Numbers over adjectives. "2,847 roofs" > "decades of experience."
- Second person. Their words for pain, not ours.
- No AI slop: no "game-changer," no "seamless," no "unlock," no "elevate."
- Read aloud. Wouldn't say it to their face → doesn't ship.
- Pre-deploy checklist: `WEB_DESIGN_BIBLE.md` → Copy Correctness Checklist.
